# Lancer Custom Content

Modifications to the base LANCER rules for my games.
![](Content/valessiobrito-Space-Pioneers-135.svg)

## Rules

The following are modifications to the base [LANCER](https://massifpress.com/lancer) core book rules. Missions are a single session, with that in mind there are some updates:

### Mech Combat

- After selecting a deployment location every LANCER may make a movement (following all normal rules) up to their movment value.
- Every frames base repair cap is halved (rounding up). Still add any additional repair cap for HULL.
- SITREPS are 4 turns instead of 6 by default.

### Downtime

- Start every session asking each LANCER "What have you been up to", and apply an appropriate downtime activity.

### Companies

- Your characters may be part of a persistent company of a few LANCERs that sometimes get together to do jobs.
- Companies have a **Hold** which stores any EXOTIC equipment recovered from missions and any other materials they might accumulate.
